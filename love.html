For You, Nikitha ❤️

I know we haven’t spoken for a month, and every day without you has felt heavier than the last. I won’t pretend I’m okay — I miss you in the quiet, I miss you in the noise, I miss you in ways words never learned to explain. If I hurt you, I’m sorry. If I failed you, I own it. But my love for you never left — it stayed, even when you were gone. Every tear I cried was your name. Every prayer I whispered was for us. I don’t want perfection. I don’t want to win. I just want one last chance to protect you, choose you, and love you better than before. If you allow it, I want to spend this Valentine’s Day not proving anything — just loving you, honestly, for the rest of my life.
Always yours.
